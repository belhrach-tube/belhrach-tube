## 🏫 About Belhrach School

**Belhrach School** is an educational digital platform dedicated to **Belhrach Saïd Secondary School**, designed to bring students, teachers, and educational resources together in one place.

Inspired by the YouTube experience, the platform provides an organized environment where educational content can be shared, explored, and accessed easily.

Our mission is to make learning more accessible, interactive, and connected through technology.

## 🚀 Platform Features

- 🎬 **Educational Videos:** A video-sharing experience dedicated to school content.
- 👨‍🏫 **Teacher Channels:** Dedicated channels for teachers to publish and organize their educational content.
- 📚 **Lessons & Summaries:** Educational lessons, explanations, and revision materials.
- 💻 **Programming Resources:** Programming tutorials, documents, and learning resources.
- 📁 **Educational Files:** A collection of useful documents and learning materials.
- 🌐 **Digital Learning:** A unified space connecting students, teachers, and educational content.
